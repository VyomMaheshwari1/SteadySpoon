STEADYHAND V3.9 - DIRECT SCREWS FOR THE SPOON CARRIAGE

REPRINT ONLY THIS PART FOR THE SCREW-HOLE FIX:
  V3_9_integrated_moving_carriage.stl

The two spoon-carriage attachment holes are now 2.6 mm pilots. Use two ordinary
M3 x 10 mm machine screws. Pass each screw through the existing pitch-horn
adapter, then slowly screw it directly into the new carriage. Stop when snug.
No heat-set inserts or nuts are needed at this connection.

The existing V3.8 pitch-horn adapter, handle tray, lid, and outer frame remain
compatible. Do not reprint them solely for this change.
